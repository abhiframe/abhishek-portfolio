About

I'm Abhishek, a motion designer and video editor working with YouTube creators and podcasters who care about pacing as much as story. My process starts with the cut, not the effect — every animation, transition, and sound decision exists to serve the narrative, not distract from it. Over the years I've edited standout animation pieces, long-form documentaries, podcast episodes, and short-form reels, learning what keeps an audience watching in each format. I keep the toolkit simple and the output consistent: fast turnarounds, clear communication, and edits that feel considered rather than rushed.
